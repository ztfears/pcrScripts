To add either a gen or spec prefix to the beginning of your FNA files, you can use the following (replacing the species placeholder):

for f in *.fna; do
	if [[ "$(head -n 1 $f)" == *species* ]]; then
		mv "$f" "spec$f";
	else
		mv "$f" "gen$f";
	fi;
done



To relabel a portion of your genomes to include more species under your "spec" group (such as when dealing with complexes), you can use the following (replacing the species placeholder):

for file in gen*; do
	[ -f "$file" ] || continue;
	if head -n 1 "$file" | grep -q "species"; then
		mv "$file" "${file/gen/spec}";
	fi;
done
